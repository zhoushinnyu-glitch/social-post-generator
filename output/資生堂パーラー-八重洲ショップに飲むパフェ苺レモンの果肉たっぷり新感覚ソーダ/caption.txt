A drinkable parfait just dropped in Tokyo 🥤
Shiseido Parlour's new "TOKYO Soda Parfait" is a next-level soda loaded with juicy fruit, vanilla ice cream, and whipped cream on top. Available in Strawberry and Lemon, it transforms from a fruity float into a creamy shake as you stir. Only at the Yaesu Shop inside Tokyo Station B1F, from May 13–31, 2026. Priced at ¥1,200 dine-in or ¥972 takeout — limited quantity daily, so go early!

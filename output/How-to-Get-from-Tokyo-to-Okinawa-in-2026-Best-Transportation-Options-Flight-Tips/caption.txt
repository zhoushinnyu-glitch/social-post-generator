Tokyo to Okinawa is one of Japan's most iconic trips — and knowing how to get there smartly makes all the difference ✈️

Over 1,600 km separates these two destinations, so flying is the only real option for most travelers. Haneda Airport is your best bet — just 20–30 minutes from central Tokyo via the Keikyu Line or Tokyo Monorail. Full-service carriers offer checked bags and comfort, while LCCs keep costs low if you pack light. Book 1–2 months ahead to score the best fares, especially outside Golden Week or Obon.

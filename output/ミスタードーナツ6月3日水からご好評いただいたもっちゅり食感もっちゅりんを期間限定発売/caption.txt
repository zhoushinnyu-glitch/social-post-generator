Mister Donut's squishiest donuts are back — and strawberry just joined the party! 🍓

Mochyurin, the fan-favorite donut known for its uniquely soft yet springy "mochyuri" texture, is returning to Mister Donut for a limited time starting June 3. Last year these sold out fast, so this time they're opening pre-orders early through the Misdo Net Order app.

① Mochyurin Kinako — classic roasted soybean sugar, simple and delicious
② Mochyurin Mitarashi — sweet-savory mitarashi filling that pairs perfectly with the chewy dough
③ Mochyurin Ichigo — NEW! strawberry filling, strawberry sugar topping, whipped cream, the works

If you missed them last year, don't sleep on the pre-order this time. The strawberry one especially won't be around long.

📌 Event: Mochyurin Limited Release
🗓 Date: Pre-orders start May 14 (Thu) at 7AM / In-store sales begin June 3 (Wed)
📍 Location: Mister Donut locations nationwide (some exclusions apply)
🚃 Access: Please check the official website for directions.

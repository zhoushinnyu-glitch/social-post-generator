Pikachu is taking over a luxury hotel in Tokyo this summer! ⚡
Grand Hyatt Tokyo in Roppongi is launching a Pokémon 30th anniversary collab featuring a flying Pikachu peach parfait, an all-yellow burger set, and a stunning mango cake — all with Pikachu's signature charm. Guests and non-guests alike can enjoy these limited menu items across two restaurants and the Fiorentina Pastry Boutique. There's even a Pokémon-themed stay option surrounded by 30 Pikachu. Only 20 servings daily, so reservations are a must!
